About Me

I am an astrophysicist interested in understanding galaxy formation and evolution in the early Universe. My research focuses on high-redshift galaxies, particularly Lyman-alpha emitters (LAEs), and the role of gas kinematics and radiative transfer in shaping their observable properties.

During my PhD at Macquarie University, Australia, I investigated the properties of Lyman-alpha emitting galaxies using integral-field spectroscopy from VLT/MUSE, complemented by observations from facilities including JWST, Subaru and HST.

My research interests include the circumgalactic medium, gas inflows and outflows, ionising photon escape, and galaxy evolution during the epoch of reionisation.

I will be joining Tohoku University, Japan, as an Assistant Professor (Research) in February 2027. There, I look forward to pursuing further studies of high-redshift galaxies using JWST, Roman and PFS.

Research Interests

  • Lyman-alpha emitters and radiative transfer
  • Galaxy formation and evolution at high redshift
  • Gas kinematics and the circumgalactic medium
  • Gas accretion, outflows and ionising photon escape
  • Cosmic reionisation and the early Universe
